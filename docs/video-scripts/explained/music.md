### The layout 

The layout of the music room is comprised of three different sections. 

- The first section is the **navigation heading**, where you can return back home, visit the templates, or go to your profile. 
- The second section is the **music finder** where you search and filter music posts. 
- And finally, the third section are the **music cards** containing the music posts. 

### Searching and Filtering

The Music Finder search is quite good. You can specify an artist name, a music genre, or part of the title. Let's see it in action.

- First let's enter part of an artist's name: `hoz` As you can see, it filtered it down to two posts belonging to Hozier. 
- Let's clear that search and now enter the word `pro`.  
  - In the music card below, it found two entries: one for the protest years, which matched on the title, but then it also found a music playlist. Why did it find that?
  - Let's view the post by double-clicking it to open it in the music view.  
  - In another video I'll go into more detail about how to create a music playlist but for now **let's just scroll down** the music view. 
    - We see that in the tags section there's a subgenre of progressive and that's what it matched on. 
    - It's not just a simple title search but it also searches other music metadata.

- In the family reaction section, you can see that a number of people have expressed a love reaction for this post twice, in fact.

- Below the reactions, we can see that a member has posted a comment on Julio's music post. 

- At the bottom of the music view is a section for discussion threads.
