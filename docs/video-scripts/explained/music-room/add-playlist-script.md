#### So, what a music room playlist? 

In the My Family Social music room a member can create different types of posts, and one of those types is a music room playlist.

The music room playlist is exactly that. It allows a family member to compose a playlist of songs to share with the family from their Spotify account. **More about Spotify in a minute**. 

The music room playlist runs in Spotify on your device. This allows you to play the music room playlist on your phone, on a Bluetooth device, or a Wi-Fi speaker; whatever your Spotify player can connect to.

#### So, why use it? 

First of all, it's easy to create in My Family Social's music room. 

If you heard some songs from an artist or a certain playlist that you want to share with family You can provide notes and comments about the playlist and do so in the privacy of your family.

It is not designed to take the place of Spotify playlists, but just as a means of sharing a small number of songs with your family.

### Slide 2

- Spotify is currently the only shared music service supported in My Family Social.  
  - Why? Spotify is the leading music streaming service with 34% of the market share with 760 million users globally.
  - The runner-up is a music streaming service called Tencent. 
  - The Apple Music service came in with a respectable 100 million subscribers globally 
    - Once the dust settles, Apple Music will be added. 
- If you don't have Spotify, then music playlists would be unavailable to you in My Family Social. 

- However, if you have Spotify, the next question is what Spotify accounts are supported? 
  - Spotify has four different premium accounts, and all of the premium accounts will work nicely in My Family Social. 
  - The Spotify free account however, will not work and will not work for several reasons:
    - There's no playback of a music playlist in My Family Social.
    - There's no way to share songs in a free account in a My Family Social playlist.

- So if you have Spotify, press on in this video.
