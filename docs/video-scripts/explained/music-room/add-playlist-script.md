#### So, what a music room playlist? 

In the My Family Social music room a member can create different types of posts, and one of those types is a music room playlist.

The music room playlist is exactly that. It allows a family member to compose a playlist of songs to share with the family from their Spotify account. **More about Spotify in a minute**. 

The music room playlist runs in Spotify on your device. This allows you to play the music room playlist on your phone, on a Bluetooth device, or a Wi-Fi speaker; whatever your Spotify player can connect to.

#### So, why use it? 

First of all, it's easy to create in My Family Social's music room. 

If you heard some songs from an artist or a certain playlist that you want to share with family You can provide notes and comments about the playlist and do so in the privacy of your family.

It is not designed to take the place of Spotify playlists, but just as a means of sharing a small number of songs with your family.

## Draft Badge
What you should know about draft mode is that it reminds the member who published this in draft mode that it's not yet published and visible to other family members. What I mean by that is, if another family member comes to visit the music room right now, that member would not see this entry because only the member who posted the draft entry would see it. 

As I said, it's a not-so-subtle reminder to the member who posted it that he or she needs to finish it up. The member will now select Edit Music, add the remaining songs, and change the status from Draft to Published. God damn it.
