Rock, Classic


Some of my favorite memories were driving around Arizona in my convertible in the late 70s, listening to music on cassette tapes. To play music in my car I had to copy the music from vinyl records to the cassette. 



I still have all of my cassettes from those days. This one is Cassette #33 with the likes of Tom Petty, Foreigner, Supertramp, Dire Straits, and Loverboy. Trust me, it wasn't easy compressing all these songs to a 60-or 90-minute cassette. 

 

I've whittled the playlist here down so you can get get a better sense for what is on the cassette. 



So, If you have Spotify, give the playlist a listen. I think you'll like it. 👍❤️
