Rock, Classic


Some of my favorite memories were driving around Arizona in my convertible in the late 70s, listening to music on cassette tapes. To play music in my car I had to record the music from vinyl records to cassette. 



I still have all of my cassettes from those days. This one is cassette #33: Tom Petty, Foreigner, Supertramp, Dire Straits, Loverboy. Trust me, it wasn't easy compressing all these songs to a 90-minute cassette.

 

If you have Spotify, give the playlist a listen. I think you'll like it.
