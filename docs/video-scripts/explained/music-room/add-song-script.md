### The layout 

The layout of the music room is comprised of three different sections. 

- The first section is the **navigation heading**, where you can return back home, visit the templates, or go to your profile. 
- The second section is the **music finder** where you search and filter music posts.

- In My Family Social, where ever something can be posted, there's almost always a template available that can be used as a starting point.

In the Music Templates page you see two templates.
1. The first is a `global` template, which is provided by My Family Social for all families to use. 
2. Below that is a `custom family` template created by a family member. 

- Global music templates are not editable by families. 
- Whereas the family templates are editable but only editable by the person who created the template. 

- Templates are only starting points; you don't have to use a template when you create a music post. If you have a different format in mind, then go ahead and create your own custom family template.

### Draft Status 
On the Music Room homepage, notice the album image was retrieved automatically, but perhaps the most important thing is that the music card is showing a draft status badge which is displayed prominently on the homepage.

There are a couple things you should know about a draft post. 
- Only the person who created the draft can see the draft post. 
- Which means other family members in the Music Room can't see it until it is published. 

In My Family Social, any post in draft status is hidden from other family members. 
- If you want other family members to see it, make sure it is `published`. 

For George it's a reminder to finish his post. 
- George will then select the **edit** button to finalize his song post.
