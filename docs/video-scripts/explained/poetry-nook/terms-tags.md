## Tags and terms
Before George writes a review of a poem, he he first comes to this page to look over a couple of things:

1. The **tags** that he will associate with the poem that he's going to review.

2. He'll do a quick recap review of the poetry **terms** that he may wish to include in his review.

On the page, there are four categories of tags. The little tag icon identifies them very cleverly. 

tags are things that we will associate with a poem. Let's expand the **poetic theme** tag.

If you're unsure about what a particular term is in the poetic theme category, you can **expand it to see a definition**. 

In my opinion, the **terms category** really shines in this regard. 

I came across the term **anaphora** recently and found a definition of it in the terms section. 

See also: **epistrophe** 

Since there are a lot of terms in both the tags and terms categories, the finder is an invaluable tool. Let's just enter a simple term like "verse" to see what shows up. 

It found that wording in three category entries. 

**Click on the X** to remove the search term. 

Let's **go back to the poetry homepage **and take a look at how the tags are used when posting a new poem. 

## Add poem
