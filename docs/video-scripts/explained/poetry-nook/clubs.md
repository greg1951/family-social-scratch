## Clubs-home-page 
Many families have been in book clubs and some in poetry clubs, but the goal is the same: for people to read a book or a poem and then get together to discuss it. 

Well, how do book or poetry clubs work on My Family Social? 

It requires participation by the family members. My Family Social will provide a framework around which book or poetry club members can be schedule a club session, but then it's really up to the members to first read the book or poem and then spend some time to discuss it, you know, like a real book or poetry club.
