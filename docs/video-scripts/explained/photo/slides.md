When you go to the My Family Social Galleries, you're actually going to the first of two galleries, which is the Family Gallery. 

### Family Gallery

- The **Family Gallery** is where family members share pictures with other members of the family.

- This page consists of photo albums that family members have shared.

- You won't find any buttons in the Family Gallery to upload pictures or create albums. That's done in the **Member Gallery**. 

### Member Gallery

- The Member Gallery is only accessible by the logged-in member. Other members cannot see another member's photo gallery. 

- In the member gallery, the member can upload photos and create albums. 

- The member can add descriptions to each of their pictures and and add pictures to an album.  

- On the album, there's an option to share the album and its pictures with the family. 

- If selected, the album would show up in the Family Gallery that can be shown to the family.

### Edit album

- By double-clicking an album, the album is opened in edit mode. 

- The dialog is a little busy, but you see the description at the top for the album. 

- Then you see the checkbox that, when checked, will allow this album to be seen in the Family Gallery.

- Below that is a scroll strip for the photos that are in the current album, and they can be removed individually. 

- Scrolling down to the bottom, we see now the scroll strip for the unallocated photos (that is, the ones that were uploaded but not in an album yet). These photos can be added to the album.

- Let's cancel the edit dialog.

### View album
- Now, I'll simply **select the album** (San Juan) to show the current photos in the album.

- When photos are uploaded into the member gallery, they are either flagged as being in portrait or in landscape mode. 

- What this allows you to do is to arrange the pictures in your album in a way that best optimizes how they will be shown in the album frame. 

- As a general rule, I could have four portrait photos on one row, or I could have three landscapes on one row or I could have two portrait and two landscape on one row. 

- Combining portrait and landscape photos on a row makes for a more interesting presentation of your photos in the album.

- You may not remember these rules so if you were to go to the online help, it will take you to the **Member Gallery context help**. The very first text help topic will help you to remember these guidelines.

### Private album 
- If you were working on albums and the album is not in a state that's ready for sharing, a very simple thing to do is to simply not share that album with the family. 
- Doing this will keep the album from being seen in the Family Gallery. 

- You could be waiting on pictures from other family members that you want to add to your album. When you get them, you upload them and add them in. Then when ready, edit the album again and select the share option. 

- I will go ahead and edit the Texas Wildflower album and turn off that share option, and then return back to the Family Gallery.
