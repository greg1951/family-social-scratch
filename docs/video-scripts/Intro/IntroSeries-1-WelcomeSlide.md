## For Families and Friends
*My Family Social* is all about what families do in this **virtual** world we live in. 

Families theses days largely communicate online, sending pictures and messages online, most of the content is spread around to many different digital platforms.

- If you think about how we share things however, most of the content is spread around to many different digital platforms. 

- If you want to share pictures, or an interesting book you've read recently, or one of your mom's favorite recipes, **is there a platform that supports ALL of that?** 

## All in One Place

**Yes**. It's *My Family Social*. It's where your family and family friends, **your inner circle** can go to share all of those things, all in one place.

- My Family Social is dedicated to the notion that **what families members and family friends do** online should be at the **family level** and should be **private and secure**.

## Data Privacy
- My Family Social was built from the ground up with the family's data privacy in mind.
- When you login you must provide a family name as a part of your credential. Without it, you can't get in.
- The content posted in your family is **only visible** to members of your family. Members in your family can only interact with members of your family and not with other families. 
  - The content and access is isolated to the family unit.
- The content in your family cannot be seen or shared with other families on My Family Social.

# The My Family Social House

Let's move to the next slide that shows the My Family Social home; Each of the families registered in My Family Social have heir own home, and in their home there are different things the members of the family can do. It's a big fun house.   

## The Ground Floor

In the My Family home, there are ten rooms on three levels, so let's start at the beginning on the ground floor. 
What a better place to start than the kitchen? Everybody hangs out in the kitchen. 

- The kitchen of course is where the foodies in the family will share their best recipes with each other. 
  - Wouldn't it be nice if your family recipes were in one place, for everyone to enjoy?
  - The guided tour is first going to visit the kitchen because that is the feature in the house where I believe many members will find good value. 

When you leave the kitchen you might pass through the Photo Hallway. 
- Here's where the family can shared their **family pictures**, all in one place for everyone to see 

- Who emails anymore? How about all of those spam text messages you get? 
  - The Mailbox isn't intended to replace emails and text messaging but what it does it centralize some of the messaging within your inner circle.

- The **Living Room** is where people hang out and chat about world events or tell incredible family stories. 
  - A cool feature in this room are the video blogs.

## The Middle Floor

In the center of the middle floor is where you'll find the Game Room. 

- There are currently eight games supported in the Game Room, from Mexican Train and Acquire (my family favorites), monopoly. triominos, to Dart games like Cricket. 
  - It's not just score keeping but game history, leader boards and player stats are kept for each of the games. 
  - There's no more arguing about who won the last time you played. 

- The **Music Salon** is a place to share your favorite albums, songs, song lyrics and **playlists**. 

- In a family there **may be an aspiring poet who doesn't yet know it** in their midst. 
  - The Poetry Nook is not for everyone, as it is a very technical art form but the My Family Social poetry feature is just excellent. 
  - You can start or join a poetry club and discuss your favorite poetry.

## The Top Floor
Let's hit the stairs and go up to the top floor of the My Family Social house.

- The **TV Room** is where members can share their favorite TV shows with family and friends and get their take on it.

- Maybe put on a **movie** and please pass the popcorn would ya? 
  - If you've seen a movie recently that you liked, how about posting a review In the **Movie Theater**?
  
- Have you read a good **book** or a **poem** recently and want to share that? 
  - You can even start a book or poetry club on My Family Social for the more serious readers.

## Summary
Before we go on a video tour of the house, let me summarize **what is My Family Social**

- It's oriented to what family and their family friends do
- It's a safe place where the family can find put their pictures, recipes, reviews of all sorts, play games, and post blogs. 

The tour of My Family Social consists of various separate videos, to keep things short; we know how valuable your time is.
