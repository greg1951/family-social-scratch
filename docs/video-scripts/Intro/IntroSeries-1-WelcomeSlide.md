## Show the My Family Home slide

Each of the families registered in My Family Social have their own family home, and in their home there are different things the family can do.

- Families watch TV shows, go to the movies, read books, listen to music, play games, post pictures, share recipes, and sit and talk. 

- *My Family Social* Provides families with a home where they can share all of those experiences in the privacy of their family.  

- Families theses days largely communicate online, sending pictures and messages online, most of the content is spread around to many different digital platforms.

- If you want to share pictures, or an interesting book you've read recently, or one of your mom's favorite recipes, **is there a platform that supports ALL of that?** 

  - **Well, of course**. It's *My Family Social*. It's where your family and family friends, **your inner circle** if you will, can go to share all of those things, all in one place.

- My Family Social is dedicated to the notion that **what families members and family friends do** online should be at the **family level** and should be **private and secure**.

---

## Show the My Family landscape slide

There are many families on My Family Social. Think of it as a large virtual neighborhood where families can go. However, a family member in one house cannot walk into any of the other houses in the neighborhood. 

---

## Show the My Family privacy slide
 
- My Family Social was built from the ground up with the family's data privacy in mind.
- The content posted in your family is **only visible** to members of your family. Members in your family can only interact with members of your family and not with other families. 
  - The content and access is isolated to the family unit.
- The content in your family cannot be seen or shared with other families on My Family Social.
