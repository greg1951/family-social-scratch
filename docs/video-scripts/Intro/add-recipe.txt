I borrowed Natasha’s recipe and altered it to add a smidge of garlic powder and rosemary. These potatoes are easy to make as a side for many meals.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eCdJSRX9s_0

Serves: 4, Prep Time: 10 minutes, Cook Time: 30 minutes

Ingredients
1-2 potatoes, peeled, rinsed, and sliced into bite size pieces
2 tbsp olive oil
1 ½ tsp kosher salt
1 tsp ground pepper corn
1 tsp garlic powder
Pinch of rosemary

Cooking Steps
Preheat the oven to 425℉.
Slice the potatoes into bite size pieces, rinse, and dry.
Combine all of the ingredients In a medium size bowl and mix thoroughly.
Cover a baking pan with a sheet of aluminum foil.
Spread the ingredients evenly on the baking pan.
Put in the oven for 15 minutes. (Be sure to set a timer!)
Flip the potatoes over and bake for another 10 minutes (again, with a timer).
Remove and garnish with rosemary.
