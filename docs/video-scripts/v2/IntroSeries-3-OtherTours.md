## Show Finder 

- The **Show Finder** let's you quickly find a TV show by entering anything about the tv show, be it **part of the show name**, the **streaming channel**, or even the **family member** who posted the show review. 

- As you can see, it's really fast and accurate and avoids having to scroll through the shows.

- The **Start** and **End dates** filter defaults to a three month range but you could go back earlier if you wish.

- You can enter **comments** on any of the shows (**as you see Julio did**) but comments are not collaborative, meaning you cannot reply to a comment.

- Discussion Threads however are collaborative so members can have a discussion about any subject, be it a TV show, movie, recipes.

- If you want to filter the list to show only TV shows that have discussion groups, click on the checkbox. 

    **Open Sunny in Phil.** Discussion Thread and **Expand All**

## Scroll Strip

- In any of the cards, be it the Finder cards or the scroll strip, if you double-click a card you can see the show's review.

  - Double-click on **Shrinking**: point out you can upload images or post links about the show. 

  - Notice in the Reactions at the bottom there is one Love reactions and a comment posted.

  - There are two scroll strips: one for the **latest** and the other for the **top rated** shows.

## Show Templates

## Cricket

The next game we'll look at is the **Cricket game** which is actually a darts game frequently played in bars and taverns (or, so I've heard).

1. Select **Cricket** ... I'll select a game of cricket that is **currently in progress** ... **Cricket-Dripping Dartsters**
2. The game can be played by 2 or 4 players, where 4 players are on two teams. 
3. Each side (or team) takes turns throwing the darts.
4. They record their darts in a ledger of three darts.
5. When they submit their dart scores their team scoreboard will be updated automatically. 
6. Because the game is played in the presence of alcoholic beverages, the scoreboard helpfully reminds them which side is to throw darts next. 

    Let's look at one more game: **Acquire**
