## For Families and Friends
*My Family Social* is all about what families do in this **virtual** world we live in. 

If your family is anything like mine, it is spread out everywhere across the country. 

Although we get together on some major holidays and occasional family events, it never seems like it's often enough. 

- Although we're remote from one another, we communicate by sharing pictures, message online. 

- If you think about how we share things, most of the content we share is fractured and spread around to many different digital platforms. 

- If you want to share some pictures of family or an interesting book you've read recently, or one of your mom's favorite recipes, **is there a platform that supports ALL of that?** 

## All in One Place

**Yes**. It's *My Family Social*. It's where your family and family friends, **your inner circle** can go to share all of those things, all in one place.

- My Family Social is dedicated to the notion that **what families members and family friends do** online should be at the **family level** and should be **private and secure**.

## Data Privacy
- My Family Social was built from the ground up with the family's data privacy in mind.
- When you login you must provide a family name as a part of your credential. Without it, you can't get in.
- The content posted in your family is **only visible** to members of your family. Members in your family can only interact with members of your family and not with other families. 
  - The content and access is isolated to the family unit.
- The content in your family cannot be seen or shared with other families on My Family Social.

# The My Family Social House

This is the My Family Social house. There are parts of the house where the family does things. 

## The Ground Floor


- There's the **kitchen** table where most people hang out and share delicious food and recipes. 
  - Wouldn't it be nice if your family recipes were in one place, for everyone to enjoy?


- How about sharing **family pictures** and having those in one place for everyone to see? 
  - The galleries in My Family Social are quite good. 

- Who emails anymore? How about all of those spam text messages you get? 
  - The Mailbox isn't intended to replace those things but what it does it centralize some of the messaging you do with your family.

- The **Living Room** is where people hang out and chat about world events and some of the incredibly funny things we've done as a family. 
  - The living room provides place where you blog your favorites stories and share them with those close to you. 

## The Middle Floor

- Since most family play games at the kitchen table, we've located the **Game Room** right above the kitchen. 
  - There are currently eight games supported in the Game Room, everything from Mexican Train to Dart games like Cricket. It's not just score keeping but game history, leader boards and player stats are kept for each of the games. 
  - There's no more arguing about who won the last time you played. 

- The **Music Room** is a place to share your favorite songs, song lyrics and albums. You can even add playlists to your musical posts. 

- A family **may have a poet and don't know it** in their midst. 
  - The Poetry Nook is not for everyone, as it is a very technical art form. 
  - But you will have a hard time finding a better poetry website for sharing poems than what My Family Social provides. 

## The Top Floor
- A little bit of **television** anyone? The **TV Room** is where members can share their favorite TV shows with family and friends and get their take on it.

- Maybe put on a **movie** and please pass the popcorn would ya? 
  - If you've seen a movie recently that you liked, how about posting a review In the **Movie Theater**?
  
- Have you read a good **book** or a **poem** recently and want to share that? 
  - You can even start a book or poetry club on My Family Social for the more serious readers.

## Summary
In conclusion: 
- My Family Social is for a family and their family friends. 
- It's a safe place where the family can find put their pictures, recipes, reviews of all sorts, play games, and post blogs. 
- Strong login rules and encryption ensure the family data are **secure**, and **only visible** by members within the family.
