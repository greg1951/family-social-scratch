# Overview
After recording videos in OBS, you use shotcut to clean up and, once that's done, merge the videos into the final video to be posted. 

## Intro music

The [pixabay.com](https://pixabay.com/) site is a good place to go for stock music, which I'm using short clips in the slide intros in all of my family social videos. A number of MP3s have been downloaded already into the `C:\Users\gregh\Downloads\mp3s` folder. 

1. When the final shotcut video is being produced, create a separate audio track in shotcut and slide in a background soundtrack MP3.

2. Add the **Limiter audio filter** to the music soundtrack and lower the volume because it's way too loud. 

## Video Audio filter 
After you drag in your first actual video clip into your shotcut project, select the video audio track heading and add a limiter to it. The limiter is going to reduce spikes in high spoken volume as well as elevate volume for parts of the speech that are not very loud.
